# Pricing Strategy — PaperViz

> Last updated: 2026-08-29
> Experiment duration: 30 days
> Success threshold: 5% of free users click upgrade at least once

## Packages

### Free
- **Price:** $0/month
- **Limit:** 5 papers/month
- **Features:** Basic analysis, simplified text, chapter charts
- **Target:** Acquisition — let users experience value before asking for payment

### Pro
- **Price:** $29/month
- **Limit:** 50 papers/month
- **Features:** Everything in Free + higher volume
- **Margin:** 52% at $0.30/paper cost
- **Target:** Active researchers who need regular paper analysis

### Research
- **Price:** Contact us (no fixed price)
- **Limit:** Custom (typically 200+ papers/month)
- **Features:** Everything in Pro + priority processing, custom support
- **Margin:** Negotiated per customer
- **Target:** Labs, research groups, high-volume users

## Experiment Design

### Metrics to Track
1. **Pricing page views** — Total visits to /pricing
2. **Engaged views** — Time on page >10 seconds
3. **CTA clicks** — Clicks on upgrade buttons
4. **Email captures** — Waitlist signups

### Success Criteria
- **5% upgrade click rate** from free users = pricing resonates
- **2-5% = marginal** — needs iteration
- **<2% = pricing or value prop problem** — rethink

### Timeline
- **Days 1-7:** Baseline measurement
- **Days 8-14:** First iteration if needed
- **Days 15-30:** Full experiment run
- **Day 30:** Evaluate and decide on payment integration

## Upgrade Flow
- No payment processing yet (future chunk)
- "Upgrade" shows waitlist/email capture
- Transparent "Coming Soon" messaging
- Track intent, not transactions
