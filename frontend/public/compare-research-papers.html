The problem

Manual comparison is slow and error-prone

Comparing research papers means reading both fully, then mentally mapping findings, methods, and conclusions. For literature reviews, meta-analyses, or just trying to understand a debate, this is the bottleneck.

  • Reading two papers takes 4–8 hours for non-specialists
  • Easy to miss subtle disagreements buried in discussion sections
  • Methodological differences often explain conflicting results — but are hard to spot
  • Literature reviews require comparing dozens of papers, not just two

The solution

Automated comparison with evidence

PaperViz analyzes both papers and produces a structured comparison. You see agreements, disagreements, and methodological differences — each linked to the source text.

  • Side-by-side comparison across research question, method, findings, and conclusions
  • Automatic detection of agreements and disagreements
  • Methodology comparison highlighted separately
  • Each comparison point linked to the original paper for verification

What you get

PaperViz comparison

Areas of Agreement

Both agree Sleep quality affects cognitive performance. Both studies found statistically significant effects of sleep deprivation on working memory and attention.

Areas of Disagreement

Conflict Effect size differs. Paper A reports a large effect (Cohen's d = 0.82) while Paper B reports a moderate effect (d = 0.41). The difference may stem from Paper A using a sleep lab while Paper B used self-reported sleep quality.

Honest limitations

Current limitations

  • Works with text-layer PDFs only — no scanned/image PDFs yet
  • Comparison is automated interpretation, not expert review
  • Papers with very different topics may produce weak comparisons
