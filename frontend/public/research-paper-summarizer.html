The problem

Academic papers are hard to read

Research papers are written for experts. Dense jargon, complex methodology, and impenetrable prose make them inaccessible to students, journalists, and anyone outside the specific field.

  • Dense academic language hides the actual findings
  • Reading a single paper takes 2–4 hours for non-specialists
  • Key claims get buried in pages of methodology and literature review
  • Students spend more time decoding language than understanding science

The solution

Clear summaries, verified against the source

PaperViz reads the full paper and produces a structured summary in plain language. Every claim is verified against the original text — so you can trust what you're reading.

  • Structured sections: Research Question, Method, Findings, Limitations
  • Claim verification checks each finding against the source paper
  • Choose your reading level: Simplified or ELI5
  • Charts and figures re-visualized for clarity

Honest limitations

What PaperViz can and can't do

Current limitations

  • Works with text-layer PDFs only — no scanned/image PDFs (OCR not supported yet)
  • Summaries are interpretations, not replacements — always check the original paper
  • Share links expire after 7 days of inactivity
  • Charts are re-visualized representations, not exact replicas
